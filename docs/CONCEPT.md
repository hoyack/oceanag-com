# OceanAG concept brief

## Audience and problem
Growers, co-ops, and agricultural operators often receive weather, labor, equipment, inventory, and market signals in separate systems. The problem is not a lack of dashboards; it is translating mixed evidence into an accountable next action.

## Positioning
OceanAG is the practical agricultural operations intelligence layer: field notes and focused systems thinking for teams that run the real work. It avoids commodity AgTech buzzwords, fake research claims, and marine/blue-economy positioning.

## Information architecture
Home → Field notes → About → Subscribe → Talk to us, supported by privacy, terms, disclosure, and corrections.

## Visual direction
An operator field memo rather than a SaaS dashboard: soil-dark surfaces, field green, harvest gold, ledger rules, asymmetric editorial composition, restrained motion.

## Content pillars
1. Decision design and exception handling
2. Traceable field context and data discipline
3. Practical automation boundaries
4. Workflows that survive weak connectivity and real operating windows

## CTA and revenue hypothesis
Primary conversion: field-dispatch subscription. Secondary: a bounded operator conversation. Initial revenue hypothesis: paid workflow diagnostics or focused pilot work after qualification; later, clearly disclosed sponsored research. No invented pricing or active affiliate offer at launch.
